This task involves implementing a feature for creating goals within the Goals and Milestones module, including a form in a side panel with relevant fields and validation.

## Permissions
- **Full Access**: Can view, create/archive, edit, and complete goals and milestones
- **Custom Access**: Customizable permissions for specific actions
- **View Only**: Can only view goals and milestones
- **No Access**: Cannot access or view the section
