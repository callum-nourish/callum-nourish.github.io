As a carer with access to a client's goals, I need to view a list of all goals for that client so I can review them and understand the milestones associated with each goal, including their timelines and progress status.

### Story 1: Goals List Access
**As a** carer with access to a client's goals  
**When I** click on the Goals button on a client's bar  
**I should** see a list of a client's goals  
**So I can** review them and understand the milestones associated to each of them

### Story 2: Goals Timeline Information
**As a** carer with access to a client's goals  
**When I** view the list of client's goals  
**I should** see their Start Date, their Target date (when relevant), and when they were last updated  
**So I can** understand the timelines expected to work on each of them with the person I support
